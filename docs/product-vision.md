# Product Vision and Scope

## What Wafflr is
Wafflr (spelled W-A-F-F-L-R) is a gamified group decision-making product designed to reduce decision fatigue and decision anxiety. It gives a group a shared, structured way to turn “What should we do?” into a decision everyone can participate in.

## Problem
Groups often get stuck because people have different preferences, hesitate to suggest options, or keep cycling through possibilities without reaching a decision.

## Promise
Wafflr lowers the effort of suggesting choices, makes preferences actionable, and gives the group a clear path to an outcome.

## Brand
The name is Wafflr. The primary mascot is Butters, a cute waffle character. Visual explorations include bright, playful food characters with character-matched limbs, distinctive sneakers, and transparent backgrounds for reusable assets. See brand-and-characters.md.
## How a room works
A room is not a live social space or multiplayer game. It is created only when the creator confirms creation, at which point it receives a room code and QR code. The creator chooses one of two paths: create every card themselves, or allow guests to submit cards independently. Guests do not see one another's work or activity, and there is no chat or presence. The creator reviews the submitted deck, starts voting, and later resolves the submitted ballots into a winner. Clients communicate through explicit submissions and persisted data, not continuous real-time synchronization.
